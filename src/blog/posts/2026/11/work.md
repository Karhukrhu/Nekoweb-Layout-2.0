I had worked 15 years at a municipal authority until they fired me this May. It's kinda a long time taking into account that I'm currently 34 years old. I had pretty much grown there, for 7 years I had the same supervisor that was absolutely gorgerous. She deffinitely knew what she was doing and what she was expecting from us. I can't say any bad words about that time. 

I had my own struggles as I depicted in the <a href="https://karhukarhu.place/blog/posts/2026/10/uni/">previous blog post</a> and at times some of my duties got too overwhelming I simply couldn't do them. My supervisor was so understanding she released me from those duties. I had told her that if I was forced to do them, I would have to quit. I mean can you really hope for a better manager?

Overall I didn't really climb up the career ladder there. I had one "promotion" that wasn't really a promotion, but a permanent employment instead of zero-hour contract which of course was way better situation for me. This blissed situation lasted as long as 7 years. Until the change negotiations took place and they saw I had no future there anymore. So I got kicked out.

I saw this coming right after they announced about the negotiations. I had had a feeling that my work doesn't really matter anymore and all that me and my co-worker did was more or less in vain. Our manager was supportive, but it didn't help. We were doomed. Before anyone knew about the negotiations, the executive management told everybody to think about what operations were such that the company wouldn't survive without. It was like digging your own grave. And our CEO cried at the annoucement event. I don't get it.

Being unemployed felt like a long vacation at first, but very soon it started to be stressful. The monetary situation has never been so bad it is now. And honestly I don't know if I can keep this household running with my current income. I bought an apartment four years ago and I would never ever do such a thing again, if I knew this had happened. Now I'm trapped with a very low income and a mortgage. 

This idleness also feels like there's no place for me in this world. Despite applying dozen of jobs, I have landed only one interview. And there are often hundreds of applications for a single job. The situation is terrible here. Basically every unemployed person has to apply for 4 jobs in a month and this system results in very very many pointless applications just to fulfill the needed 4 jobs a month. People are writing applications to positions they know they have no chance to get. And the employers need to go through all of them. At least I hope they do.

Now that I had been unemployed for 6 months, I got a fantastic chance to be part of game development! My friend tipped about me to her friend who is developing a pixel art game and I get to do the graphics for it. I'm excited and thankful for this opportunity. This is something I've never done though, I have no experience in game development, I have no idea how it works, but I'm slowly getting hang of it.

Even though this might have been my dream job pretty much, (not necessarily make graphics for games, but graphic design in general), I still feel like I'm underachieving. The problem isn't really starting the daily work, but keeping up to it. I keep breaks and after those breaks it's hard to work again. Maybe it's partly because I have only little idea what I'm doing. Also, I don't yet have a comprehensible access to see how my stuff looks like in the actual game. 

This situation has kinda made me upset which is silly as I got a job. The problem is the deadline. Everything needs to be done until December when the game launches as a demo. And there are so many things graphic wise you need to take into account, even though it's not the most complex game. I was so upset and rather stressed by this I had to contact my employer.

So it happened that I really didn't have to worry about the deadline. I was told I can make as much as I can and I don't have to push myself. After being on a partial rehabilitation benefit for the last couple of years, I'm really struggling to get 6 hrs a day done. But I have high hopes it'll get easier as the time passes. So I have yet another great supervisor, I got to concider myself lucky!
